# news-image-01 — The World Today: Friday 13 November 2026

A deterministic pure-stdlib Python program that draws one image summing up
the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026: the US Senate summoning Huang &
Lutnick, Zelensky meeting US envoys, a Ukrainian drone hitting a Russian
warship, Iran claiming a strike on a US radar site in Bahrain, the FBI on
North Korea's $400m crypto hack, Harry Kane breaking England's caps record,
and the US reporting a record 483,200 deportations in 2026.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` — writes `out/entry.png`
(1024x1024 PNG, byte-identical on every run, stdlib only, no network).

Entry for make.fun competition news-image-01 ($250, top 100 share the pool).
